Vendored, not installed: there is no `mermaid` entry in package.json and the board
fetches nothing at runtime. The bundle is inlined into DASHBOARD.html by
generate-dashboard.js (buildMermaidScript) exactly as the fonts are base64-inlined
into the stylesheet by dashboard-css.js.

Chosen deliberately over dist/mermaid.esm.min.mjs: the ESM build cannot be inlined
into a single-file `file://` page, and the .core.mjs entry point resolves its
diagram registry through bare specifiers. The IIFE build carries every diagram type
statically — verified: zero `import(` call sites — which is the property that makes
a single-file offline board possible at all.

To upgrade: download the tarball, extract `package/dist/mermaid.min.js` and
`package/LICENSE`, replace both files here, update this note, and re-run
`node _00-Project-Management/93-Scripts/smoke-dashboard.js --mermaid-walk --assert-all-rendered`.
